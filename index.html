A guide to the main areas taught across KS2. Tick a topic when she feels ready, then revisit it whenever she needs a refresher.

How to use this checklist
These are learning areas, not a prediction of the exact questions. SATs sample from the KS2 curriculum, so some topics were taught in earlier years. Focus on understanding and explaining her working, not just ticking every box.

Test papers: Maths has an arithmetic paper and two reasoning papers; English reading is a comprehension paper; Grammar, Punctuation and Spelling has questions and a spelling paper.
